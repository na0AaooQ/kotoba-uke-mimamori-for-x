1. Accuracy and completeness

This extension is a supplemental tool that uses fixed rule-based checks inside your browser to show a cushion on posts displayed on X.

It does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or usefulness of its results. A cushion may not appear when it would be helpful, and it may appear on a post that does not present a problem.

2. Your judgment and responsibility

The extension's display results are reference information intended to provide a small pause before reading.

You are responsible for deciding whether to read a post now and how to respond to any content shown on X.

3. Liability

The developer is not liable for any loss or damage arising from the use of, or inability to use, this extension, except where liability cannot be excluded under applicable law.

4. External service

This extension works on X, but it is not provided, approved, or guaranteed by X Corp. or its affiliates.

Changes to X, service availability, or outages may change how the extension appears or works, or may make it unavailable.

5. What the extension does not do

  • It does not evaluate posters.
  • It does not rate accounts or assign account-risk labels.
  • It does not automatically block or report content, and it does not delete posts.
  • It does not replace X's official features or appropriate support services or professional resources.

6. Changes, suspension, and termination

The extension's content, specifications, availability, and delivery method may be changed, suspended, or discontinued without prior notice.
